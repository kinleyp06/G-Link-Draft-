# G-Link guide for admins

Your menu: Dashboard, Booking requests, Extension requests, International booking, Block rooms/beds, Change room,
Rooms & hall, Rate table.

- **Dashboard** — counts of pending requests, guests in house, arrivals, free beds tonight, and the requests that need you.
- **Booking requests** — filter by status, guest house, or search (name, email, CID, `GL-0012`). Open one to **Review**:
  guest, stay, bill and people. **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason (the guest sees it). Approved bookings can be
  cancelled (with a reason) or moved (**Change room**). The *Meeting hall* tab has hall requests (approve / reject).
- **Extension requests** — "Room free?" tells you whether the extra nights fit. Approve is switched off when they do not.
- **International booking** — book for guests from abroad: room, dates, each guest's name, passport number and
  nationality. It is approved straight away at the International rate.
- **Block rooms/beds** — take beds out of use for dates (repairs, events). You cannot block beds that bookings already hold.
- **Change room** — enter the booking ref; only rooms with enough free beds are listed. The guest gets an email.
- **Rooms & hall** — add or edit rooms (number, type, beds), halls and guest houses. Set *Inactive* to hide one from guests.
- **Rate table** — room rate per bed per night and hall rate per day for each guest type. New rates apply to new bookings only.
- Pending requests that nobody reviews before their check-in date are cancelled automatically.
