# G-Link guide for guests

1. **Create an account** — *Create an account* on the sign-in page. Enter your email and a password
   (8+ characters, letters and numbers). Open the email from G-Link and click **Verify my email** (it works for 24 hours;
   check spam; you can ask for a new link on the sign-in page).
2. **Sign in** and open **Profile**: add your first name, last name and phone number (and CID). You cannot book without them.
3. **Rooms** — choose the guest house and your dates, press **Search**. Each room shows how many beds are free.
   *Full on these dates* means pick other dates or another room.
4. **Book** — choose your guest type (RUB Staff, RUB Student, Official, Private) and purpose.
   In a double room you can untick "share" to have the whole room.
5. **Guests** — add each person who comes with you (one bed each). Leave it empty if you come alone.
6. **Shared room** — a dormitory (or a shared double) may have other guests; tick to confirm.
7. **Bill** — check nights × beds × rate, then **Send booking request**. Your booking is *Pending* until an admin
   approves it; you get an email either way.
8. **My bookings** — see every booking and its status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, Completed).
   Open one to see the bill, payments, or to **Cancel booking** (before check-in).
9. **Stay longer** — on an approved booking, **Ask to stay longer**, choose the new date and a reason. The admin
   approves if the room is free; the bill grows by the extra nights.
10. **Meeting hall** — choose the date, time, number of people, purpose and guest type. Times already taken are shown.
11. **Pay** at the guest house when you check in (cash, bank transfer or mBoB). The incharge records it.
