# G-Link guide for incharges

Your menu: Bookings, Today, Check-in/out.

- **Bookings** — approved stays for today, this week, or all. Search by ref, name, email or CID. "Stay" shows
  *Not arrived*, *In house* or *Checked out*; "Due" shows what is still to pay.
- **Today** — who arrives, who leaves, who is in the house, anyone who should already have left, and hall events today.
- **Check-in/out** — find the booking (ref, name or CID) and **Open** it.
  - **Check in** works from the check-in date. Ask for the CID / passport of every guest listed.
  - **Record payment** — amount, method (Cash, Bank Transfer, mBoB) and the receipt / reference number
    (needed for everything except cash). You cannot record more than is due.
  - **Check out** marks the stay *Completed* and frees the beds. It warns you if money is still due.
